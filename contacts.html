Frequently Asked QueSstions

What are the best times to visit Delhi?

The best times to visit Delhi are during the cooler months from October to March.

How can I get around the city?

Delhi has a well-connected public transportation system, including buses, metro, and auto-rickshaws. Taxis and ride-sharing services are also available.

Are there any safety tips for tourists?

It's advisable to stay in well-populated areas, avoid traveling alone at night, and keep your belongings secure. Always follow local guidelines and regulations.
